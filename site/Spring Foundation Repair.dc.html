The soil

Why Spring foundations move

Spring sits on expansive clay. It swells when it's wet and shrinks during dry Texas summers, and a slab resting on it rises and settles with every cycle. Add a high water table near Spring Creek and Cypress Creek, mature oaks pulling moisture from the soil, and gutters that dump water at the slab edge, and you get the cracks most Spring homeowners call us about.

Most fixes come down to two things: support the house on piers driven below the active clay, and keep water moving away from the foundation.
