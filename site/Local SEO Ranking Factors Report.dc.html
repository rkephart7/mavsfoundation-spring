What I think

Organic-only is the right fit for rank and rent. It needs no address and no Google profile that can be suspended, and the ranking stays with your domain when the tenant changes.

3 of the top 4 factors are already built. What's left is links. That's what will decide whether you beat Angi, HomeAdvisor and the contractors' own sites for "foundation repair Spring TX".

Focus on local, relevant links. Spring and Klein chambers of commerce, local news, sponsorships, suppliers, realtors and home inspectors. 10–20 good ones beat hundreds of directory links.

Keep the content fresh. The 2026 survey added freshness of site and service-page content as factors. Small updates every month or two (a new project, an FAQ) will help.

Priorities for this site

  1. Launch, submit the sitemap in Search Console and add LocalBusiness and Service schema.
  2. Build 10–20 local links over the first 3 months.
  3. List the business consistently on the main directories (Yelp, BBB, Bing Places, Apple).
  4. Add one fresh update a month: a project write-up, photos or a new FAQ.
